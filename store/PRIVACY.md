# Privacy policy: HAR Sanitizer

Last updated: 20 October 2026

HAR Sanitizer processes your data only on your own device.

**What we collect:** nothing. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no server. It makes no network requests, and the browser enforces this through the extension's content-security policy.

**What is stored on your device:** your settings (preset, over-redaction level, custom rules, kept values, site profiles, panel size limit), using the browser's `storage` permission. The contents of HAR files and recorded requests are never written to storage. While you record in DevTools, redacted requests are held in that panel's memory until you clear them or close DevTools.

**Files you create:** sanitized files, reports and encrypted mappings are saved only where you choose to download them. Sharing them is your decision.

**Links:** the extension contains plain links to runlocaltools.online. Opening one takes you to that website, which has its own policy. No data from the extension is attached to those links.

**Permissions:** only `storage`. No access to any website, tab or browsing history.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
