HAR Sanitizer is installed

Redact cookies, tokens, passwords and personal data from network recordings before you share them. Everything stays on your device.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Open DevTools on the page with the problemPress F12 (Windows/Linux) or ⌥⌘I (Mac). The extension adds a tab there, not a toolbar button.
  2. Click the “HAR Sanitizer” tabIt sits next to Network, Console and the rest. If you do not see it, click the » at the end of the tab row. Then reproduce the problem: requests show up already redacted.
  3. Click “Share-ready ✓”It stops recording, re-scans for leftover secrets, shows a clear verdict and downloads the sanitized .har.
